Version 1.0.2 (2026-11-14)
- The Treasury's shops are now called Specials, Blueprints, Hullmod Specs, Exotics and Upgrades, so the names fit on their tabs
- Hullmod Specs and Blueprints sell one of each, and anything you already know or bought there is not offered again
- Blueprint packages leave the shop once you know everything in them
- Station Augment blueprints, RAT industry blueprints, AoTD Citadel Schematics and Exiled Sector schematics moved from Specials to Blueprints
- Specials no longer sells Ship Mastery Knowledge Constructs, Industrial Evolution forge templates or IndEvo pet cryochambers
- Exotics no longer sells chips for exotics that install from an AI core (Alpha Subcore, Subsumed Alpha, Beta and Gamma Core, Daemon Core), or Exotica's install items that the game never hands out
- Shop lists are sorted by mod, then by name

Version 1.0.1 (2026-11-10)
- The mod now links to its forum thread, so version checkers can point you there.

Version 1.0.0 (2026-11-10)
- Renamed to The Everything Shop. It is now a separate mod with its own id: it does not update Extra Submarkets, and it needs a new game
- The Gilgamesh Nebula is now the Gate of Babylon
- Chicken is gone, with her contact entry and her missions
- Chicken's Roost is now The Treasury, and its shops are Treasury - Specials, Blueprints, Modspecs, Exotics and Upgrades
- Treasury prices now drop as your standing with the Independents rises
- The LunaLib settings are now under The Everything Shop; old Extra Submarkets settings do not carry over

Version 3.2.1 (2026-11-10)
- New gold icon in mod managers (TriOS) and in LunaLib's settings.

Version 3.2.0 (2026-11-10)
- New station Chicken's Roost in the Gilgamesh Nebula, also added to existing saves when they load
- Chicken (from Chicken's Tech Shop) lives there as a contact from the start and offers her dead drop, jailbreak, smuggling and spy satellite jobs
- Chicken's Tech - Specials: every usable special item from every installed mod, 999 of each, including items that need a target: RAT hull alteration installers, Exiled Sector hull items, star charts and schematics, Industrial Evolution VPCs, consumables, forge templates and cryochambers, UAF keycards, Threat hull mod parts, the SotF Daydream Analyzer, Crab Shack Gene Splicers and Forming Fluid, Sephira Conclave items and more
- Quest items, one-off rewards, wormhole anchors, Crab Shack loot boxes and items that crash without their data are kept out (list in data/config/esm_chicken/excluded_items.csv)
- Chicken's Tech - Blueprints: every blueprint package and every rare ship, weapon and fighter blueprint
- Chicken's Tech - Modspecs: a modspec for every hull mod you don't know yet (RAT alterations are not offered as modspecs)
- Chicken's Tech - Exotics and Upgrades (only with Exotica Technologies): every exotic in every form and every upgrade at every level
- Prices on Chicken's Roost drop as your standing with Chicken rises
- AI Cores Store: now sells every AI core from every installed mod, 999 of each, instead of a fixed list
- Every new shop can be switched off in the LunaLib settings
- Replaces the Chicken's Tech Shop mod: disable it to avoid two Chickens
